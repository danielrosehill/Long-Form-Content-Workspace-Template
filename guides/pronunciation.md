## Numbers and dates

<!-- House rules, so every chapter normalises the same way. -->

- Years: "twenty twenty-six", not "two thousand twenty-six".
- Percentages: "forty percent".
- Currency: amount then unit, "twelve dollars", "three hundred shekels".
- Ranges: "five to ten", never "five dash ten".
- Version numbers: "version three point two".
