## Questions worth asking

Each of these changes the outline, the voice, or the scope. Drop any the brief
already answers.

- **Reader's starting point.** What can the reader already do? A chapter written
  for a novice and a chapter written for a practitioner are different chapters.
- **Reader's reason.** Do they read it end to end, or open it at the index when
  something breaks? This settles chapter length and cross-referencing.
- **Length.** Roughly how long, and is that a constraint or an estimate?
- **What it must not become.** The failure mode you would be embarrassed by.
- **Existing material.** Notes, transcripts, prior writing, a repository, a
  working system to document — anything the research agent should start from.
- **First person or not.** Whether your own experience is evidence in this book
  or a distraction from it.
- **What is out of scope**, and where a reader should be sent instead.
- **Facts with a shelf life.** Prices, versions, APIs, regulations — what will be
  wrong in a year, and should it be in the book at all.
