# Continuity ledger

> The work's memory. Every term, figure and claim it has established, with the
> chapter that established it.
>
> This is the mechanism by which chapter 12 knows what chapter 3 already said,
> without chapter 12's writer reading chapter 3. Keeping it current is not
> bookkeeping — it is the thing that makes parallel drafting possible.

Updated by a writer in the same turn it introduces something, and audited by the
continuity pass. `longform.py lint` warns when `longform.toml` declares a key the ledger
does not mention.
